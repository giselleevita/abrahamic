## Proposed optional comparison fields — not seeded or published

These are review drafts for the **Abraham's covenant** comparison, subject to verification of its actual attached claims. Do not paste into a published form until an editor has reviewed the supporting passages and scope.

**Shared ground (draft):** “The selected Jewish, Christian and Islamic sources each give Abraham or Ibrahim a significant role in accounts of divine promise and faithful response. This shared point of reference does not make their understandings of covenant or inheritance interchangeable.”

**Key differences (draft):** “The Torah passages connect the covenant with Abraham and his descendants, with the covenant line specified through Isaac. Paul's letters interpret Abraham's promise through faith and Christ. Quran 2:124 describes Ibrahim's testing and appointment as a leader, while qualifying the extension of God's covenant in relation to wrongdoing. These selections introduce distinct interpretations; they do not exhaust the diversity within each tradition.”

Verify Genesis 17:7, 17:19–21, Romans 4:13 and its context, Galatians 3:6–29, and Quran 2:124. Some references may not exist in the small demo seed: review the complete primary passage in an appropriately attributed edition before approval. Do not fabricate unavailable excerpts or infer source agreement from similar vocabulary.
